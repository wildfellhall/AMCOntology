# Learning design and evidence

Initial research completed 3 November 2026; source review and curriculum revision completed 4 November 2026. This is a design synthesis, not a systematic review or a claim that this particular application has been experimentally validated.

## What the evidence supports

| Source | Finding and limitation | Application decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ruitenburg et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10225-5), classroom meta-analysis, published 29 October | 53 studies and 7,303 participants; average effects were g=.37 for retrieval, .20 for spacing, .40 for interleaving. Effects varied with context and assessment. These averages do not predict an individual's AMC score. | Use cumulative review and mixed practice after initial instruction. Treat scheduling as a transparent heuristic. |
| [Murray, Horner & Göbel, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10035-1), mathematics-specific meta-analysis | Spacing had a small-to-medium benefit (g=.28, 27 studies). Retrieval versus restudy was uncertain: only seven studies and a confidence interval crossing zero. | Pair recall with conceptual teaching and unfamiliar problems. Do not market formula flashcards as sufficient for mathematical transfer. |
| [Barbieri et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09745-1), worked-example meta-analysis | Across 55 studies, worked examples had a medium average effect (g=.48). Correct examples were particularly useful; adding self-explanation prompts did not uniformly improve results. | Give complete, correct examples, revealable reasoning, and optional focused reflection. Avoid compulsory essays at every step. |
| [Rohrer et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000367), preregistered classroom trial; [IES project summary](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/awards/efficacy-study-interleaved-mathematics-practice) | Interleaved practice improved delayed test performance in seventh-grade classrooms. The study concerned selected school mathematics skills, not AMC preparation. | Require learners to select a method in mixed practice; introduce a new method with focused examples first. |
| [Zeitlhofer & Zumbach, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijer.2026.103039), sequencing experiment | In a 124-participant equation-learning study, conditions containing problem solving outperformed worked examples alone. Mixed sequencing results counsel against a universal recipe. | Every lesson includes independent application, not only demonstrations. Allow experienced students to go directly to problems. |
| [Do worked examples boost the spacing effect?, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102103), experimental study | The tested combination produced no main or interaction effects, including Bayesian support for null findings. | Do not assume that stacking individually plausible techniques guarantees additive gains. Review actual learner evidence. |
| [Yeager et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04907-7), six randomized experiments | A specific combined growth-and-stress mindset intervention improved several outcomes in studied adolescent settings. Effects differed by context. A few encouraging interface sentences are not that intervention. | Normalize difficulty without claiming that all stress is beneficial. Offer a smaller next step, a concrete hint, and the ability to stop. |
| [UT Austin on *10 to 25*, 2025](https://lifeandletters.la.utexas.edu/2025/03/the-mentor-mindset/) | Yeager's mentor mindset pairs demanding standards with support and respect for young people's agency. This source explains the book; it is not an independent efficacy trial. | Let students choose their path, inspect recommendation reasons, retry work, and see specific mathematical feedback. No leaderboards, ability labels, or punishment for missed days. |
| [FUSE public practice overview](https://fuse.prc.utexas.edu/fuse-program-overview) | Public descriptions cover memory, engaged retrieval, revision, encoding, spacing, prediction, and reframing mistakes. FUSE is a teacher-development program with human relationships and coaching; software cannot claim to reproduce it. | Explain why review is scheduled, invite predictions before manipulating diagrams, keep missed questions revisitable, and use respectful feedback. These are adaptations of public principles, not copied fellowship materials or an affiliation. |
| [Fogg Behavior Model](https://www.behaviormodel.org/) and [BJ Fogg](https://www.bjfogg.com/) | The author's behavior-design framework connects motivation, ability, and a prompt. It informs habit design rather than proving a specific learning effect for AMC mathematics. | Offer a two-minute entry action and an optional personally chosen study anchor. A session can be successful without an escalating streak or long commitment. |

## Instructional sequence

Begin with an optional short diagnostic. Its purpose is routing, not ranking: a small sample cannot locate an exact ability level. A wrong answer suggests a concept to inspect, not proof that every prerequisite is missing. Learners can skip the diagnostic, choose any lesson, or change the exam track.

Each competition study begins with a mathematical need, then explains the idea, its conditions, and why the method works across three substantial teaching sections. A relevant visual or manipulable representation connects the symbols to quantities. Two worked examples show recognition and execution; solutions can be revealed in stages. A completion task removes part of the support before a practice set varies the mathematical structure, supplies two hint levels, explains the answer, and identifies a likely error. A real released contest problem provides a bridge to authentic wording and difficulty. Optional notes preserve the learner's explanation and corrections.

Review alternates between established topics. Formula work asks learners to recognize a symbolic relation, connect it to meaning and conditions, or match it to a use; it does not substitute ordinary calculation problems. Methodology work asks which approach fits a concrete problem and why competing approaches fail. Neither drill is treated as equivalent to independently solving a full problem.

## Progress and recommendations

- Reading completion and problem-solving evidence are separate records.
- Record the question, concept, mode, correctness, help used, confidence, and timestamp locally. Hints and revealed solutions do not produce independent-success evidence.
- Show descriptive states: not started, in progress, ready to build on, and retained. Security requires multiple distinct successful items and independent evidence on more than one day; repeated clicks on a familiar answer cannot manufacture mastery.
- Confidence is self-report, shown against actual performance; it does not increase a score. An incorrect high-confidence response is useful material for revision.
- Schedule reviews with explicit day intervals, shortened after errors or help. These intervals and state thresholds are product choices, not empirically fitted memory parameters.
- Prefer overdue concepts, then observed gaps, then a competition study with appropriate competition prerequisites. Missing evidence is not a prerequisite deficit; elementary references remain available without mandatory completion. Explain the recommendation and permit an override. Prerequisite edges represent useful mathematical dependencies, not an immutable psychological ordering.
- Keep accuracy, sample size, and source of evidence visible. Do not infer an AMC score, AIME qualification probability, age, intelligence, or diagnosis.

## Resources and scope

The [MAA's current AMC description](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/) specifies 25 multiple-choice questions in 75 minutes. AMC 10 excludes advanced topics such as trigonometry; AMC 12 covers high-school mathematics excluding calculus. The course defaults to AMC 10 and adds explicit AMC 12 extension lessons. Three-level challenge labels for original items are editorial approximations, not psychometric calibrations.

[Art of Problem Solving's free resource directory](https://artofproblemsolving.com/resources) supplies Alcumus, videos, and its community wiki. The brief's “Academy of Problem Solving” is interpreted as Art of Problem Solving / AoPS Academy. AoPS wiki requests returned HTTP 403 during research, so detailed released-problem verification uses the [LIVE by Po-Shen Loh archive](https://live.poshenloh.com/past-contests), which states that it hosts problems with MAA permission. Source pages retain their original statements and diagrams; the application links to them and stores attribution and answer metadata. Availability on a free site is not treated as a license to republish its archive.

The user-supplied *Mastering AMC 10/12* (686 pages) and *OmegaLearn AMC 10/12 Crash Course* (118 pages) informed the coverage audit. Their chapters span combinatorics, algebra, number theory, geometry, and advanced topics. Extracted mathematical text contains lost fraction bars and misread symbols, so visual inspection and independent derivation take precedence over extraction. The application uses newly authored teaching prose, original problems, an explicitly attributed probability adaptation, and linked authentic practice. The supplied books remain local reference materials and are excluded from future repository publication.

## Curriculum coverage

The main course contains 18 competition studies: algebraic transformations; polynomial structure; sequence synthesis; integer constraints; congruence systems; valuations and divisors; geometric constructions; ratio geometry; circle synthesis; coordinate/lattice geometry; spatial geometry; constrained counting; combinatorial identities; probability states; invariant/extremal reasoning; inequalities and domains; logarithmic/trigonometric synthesis; and complex roots of unity. The final two are AMC 12 extensions. Each has two worked examples, a completion task, a specific interactive SVG, and 14 authored practice items plus a linked released item.

The original 50 compact lessons are now explicit prerequisite references. They preserve their graph connections and saved progress. Course and practice default to competition studies, with reference and combined views available. This distinction prevents routine exercises from being presented as the principal competition curriculum. The [curriculum audit](curriculum-audit.md) records the source anchors, observed deficiencies, and completed changes.

## Product and evaluation decisions

Use a pale cream manuscript interface with serif typography, illustrated initials, fine rules, and original SVG geometry, astronomy, botany, and optics studies. Optional blue, plum, or graphite ink provides customization. Show one recommended action prominently. Keep the full map available without making students navigate a graph to learn. Support small screens, keyboard controls, mathematical screen-reader output, and reduced motion.

Validate mathematical answers, distinct choices, graph acyclicity, track consistency, complete lesson coverage, and TeX rendering. Exercise first use, diagnosis, lessons, hints, answers, revision, review scheduling, map navigation, drills, persistence, and progress backup through a local server. Keep screenshots and reference extractions outside publishable files. Evaluate learning claims conservatively; successful software tests do not establish educational efficacy.
