# Construction patterns and coverage

These patterns were synthesized after reading the user-supplied OmegaLearn Mastering AMC 10/12 and Crash Course, the MAA scope, and released questions in LIVE's archive. They describe mathematical structure rather than reusable copyrighted wording.

| Family | Productive construction | Trap to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arithmetic and rates | Nested fractions; weighted rates; reversing successive percentage changes; equal-distance travel | Averaging speeds without time weights; applying a percent to the wrong base |
| Algebraic identities | Recover a symmetric expression from sum/product; reciprocal substitution; complete a rectangle | Expanding everything; dropping cross terms; extraneous roots |
| Quadratics and polynomials | Root sums without roots; discriminant constraints; evaluate at a root shift; remainder theorem | Wrong Vieta sign; treating a nonmonic polynomial as monic |
| Sequences | Pair endpoints; compare a sum with its shifted multiple; telescoping; recurrence from last step | Wrong first index; forgetting the last uncancelled term |
| Integer structure | Prime-exponent choices; valuations; gcd/lcm; modular cycles; factor a Diophantine equation | Counting a factor twice; canceling noninvertible residues; missing negative factors |
| Geometry | Introduce an altitude; compare same-height areas; similar triangles; chord power; coordinate placement | Length versus area scaling; a whole secant versus its exterior segment; assumed symmetry |
| Counting | Ordered slots; blocks and gaps; complement; disjoint casework; stars and bars; double counting | Treating identical objects as labeled; dividing by a varying multiplicity |
| Probability | Explicit equiprobable sample space; sequential conditional factors; area ratio; first-step states | Unequal outcomes counted equally; replacement ignored; independence assumed |
| Strategy | Parity invariant; extremal choice; pigeonhole; prove a pattern after small cases | Guessing from examples without justification; changing invariant mid-process |
| AMC 12 extensions | Log-domain constraints; trig identities; sine/cosine laws; complex rotations; binomial coefficients | Squaring introduces invalid roots; wrong angle units; indexing a binomial term |

Useful difficulty ladder: direct recognition → choose among competing structures → combine two prerequisites → impose an integer/domain restriction → reverse the direction of the question. Give the early item smaller intrinsic load, not an answer-revealing label. A late item should have a short elegant solution even when finding that solution is hard.

Worked reference observations: 2022 AMC 10A #1 evaluates from the innermost fraction outward; #2 connects constant rate with estimation; #3 compresses three relations into one variable; #5 uses a corner right triangle and rationalization; #6 requires interpreting the square root of a square as an absolute value. These are examples of decision structure, not templates to disguise by swapping names.

Sources: [MAA](https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/), [AoPS free resources](https://artofproblemsolving.com/resources), [2022 AMC 10A](https://live.poshenloh.com/past-contests/amc10/2022A), [concept index](https://live.poshenloh.com/past-contests/concepts). Verify each actual source problem separately before using its key or classification.
